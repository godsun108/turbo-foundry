What our offline missed-call demo tests

Imagine a fictional HVAC business misses a service inquiry. The prototype evaluates eligibility and messaging safeguards locally; it never calls or texts the fictional customer.

  1. Incoming event: a fabricated missed-call record reaches the decision workflow.
  2. Permission check: a fictional eligible record can proceed to a simulated follow-up decision. A missed call does not imply consent.
  3. Duplicate protection: a repeated event is suppressed so the same inquiry isn't treated as a fresh lead.
  4. Opt-out: a fictional opted-out contact is rejected instead of receiving an automated message.
  5. Human review: real provider connections, consent, customer setup, and actual delivery must be verified separately.

Evidence limit: synthetic scenarios do not establish recovered customers, ROI, actual SMS deliverability, or a production AI receptionist.
