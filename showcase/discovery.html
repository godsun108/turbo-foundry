FOUNDRY · BUSINESS DISCOVERY

Let's understand the work before automating it.

We're learning where service businesses lose time handling calls, appointments, estimates and follow-up. The same proposed platform can be adapted to different industries, but only fictional offline demonstrations exist today.

Research-stage only. No live automated calls or messages, onboarding, signup submissions, paid subscriptions, or installed services are offered. This page does not collect or transmit information.

What a first conversation covers

  1. Your industry and workflow: automotive, HVAC, plumbing, landscaping, cleaning or another service business.
  2. Your biggest repetitive task: missed inquiries, quote requests, bookings, follow-up or support questions.
  3. Current process: how your team handles the task today, without disclosing any client records.
  4. Approximate volume: an optional high-level estimate, not call logs or individual contacts.
  5. Success criteria: what measurable improvement would make further evaluation worthwhile.
  6. Boundaries: existing contact permissions, human review, privacy requirements and whether a later offline walkthrough would be useful.

There is no price or delivery commitment. We do not claim recovered leads, measured ROI, production integration or deliverability.

How to share feedback safely

General ideas: Use the public GitHub research template for non-confidential suggestions only. GitHub issues are public and may require an account.

Private inquiry: Please use an already established direct contact route. Foundry has not yet published a verified dedicated business mailbox; this page does not provide a private contact form.

Do not put names of individual customers, phone numbers, appointment details, property access information, recordings, passwords, API keys, or other confidential material in a public issue.

What happens after discovery?

We assess whether a safe fictional demonstration addresses a real problem. Any actual provider connection, communications workflow, service terms, price or paid pilot requires separate verification and written approval.

No cookies, analytics, embedded forms, external scripts or payment collection. Truth before theater. Evidence before confidence. Operational before victory.
