## Hypothesis
State one falsifiable claim.

## Objective and scope
What is being tested? What is explicitly out of scope?

## Background and evidence
List sources, prior art, assumptions, and known limitations.

## Requirements and acceptance criteria
Define measurable pass/fail thresholds before testing.

## Apparatus and materials
List hardware, firmware/software versions, sensor models, calibration state, and bill of materials.

## Safety and risk controls
Identify hazards, severity, likelihood, controls, emergency stop/abort criteria, and required qualified review. Do not begin hazardous work without approval.

## Procedure
Provide reproducible numbered steps, environmental conditions, and instrument settings.

## Raw observations
Record timestamps, units, sample counts, anomalies, and links to raw data. Preserve original data.

## Analysis
Describe calculations, uncertainty, exclusions, and deviations from protocol.

## Results
Report pass/fail against each predeclared criterion. Include negative results.

## Limitations and next steps
State what the experiment does not prove and the next smallest useful test.
