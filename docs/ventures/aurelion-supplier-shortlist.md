## Cash-flow and policy evidence
Printify Pop-Up collects customer funds, covers production/shipping and transfers net earnings via Stripe. Sellers remain responsible for taxes and legal compliance. Payout generally occurs on 15th of following month, subject to verification and first-payout holds. Returns/exchanges generally not supported except eligible defects; confirm policies before publication.
- https://help.printify.com/hc/en-us/articles/12051129478417-What-is-Printify-Pop-Up-Store
- https://help.printify.com/hc/en-us/articles/12051908094993-How-will-my-Pop-Up-Store-earnings-be-paid-out
