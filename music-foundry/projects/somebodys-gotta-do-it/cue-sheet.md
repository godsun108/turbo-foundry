# Somebody's Gotta Do It — vocal cue sheet

**Style:** wonky tech / free-form bass house  
**Target:** 127 BPM (provisional), 4/4  
**Lead:** predominantly spoken, mischievous and confidently reckless  
**Vocal support goal:** spoken and sung modes in the shared workflow; this track is spoken-led. Keep sung treatment limited to optional chant/pitched textures.

The supplied lyric draft is the source of truth. Preserve wording; edits below are performance and production cues, not lyric rewrites.

## Mix / edit notes
- Keep the lead intelligible and forward; let the bass be the chaos around it.
- Use separate takes for lead, gang responses, ad-libs and chopped phrases.
- Favor short, syncopated edits; reserve longer delay/reverb throws for section transitions.
- Keep the bridge genuinely half-time, then make the final return feel oversized.
- Export separate vocal stems and a full mix when a real audio renderer is integrated.

## Current implementation boundary
This cue sheet and project manifest document the arrangement; they do not render vocals or audio. The existing Music Foundry generator produces editable MIDI only. WAV vocal rendering, spoken synthesis, singing synthesis and stem export remain future implementation work.
