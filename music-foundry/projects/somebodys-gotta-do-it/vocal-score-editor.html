Review gate

Import vocal_score.json to begin. Your edits stay in this browser until you export.

  • Every source word must remain unchanged and in order.
  • Enter reviewed ARPABET phones; use spaces between phones, e.g. M EY1 K.
  • Onsets and durations are quarter-note beats, contiguous from zero within each phrase.
  • Enter one MIDI pitch and note type per word. Silence must be an explicit <SP> unit in a separately authored score; this editor does not insert rests.
  • Every phrase must be reviewed, and tempo must be locked, before canonical export.
