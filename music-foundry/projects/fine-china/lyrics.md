# Fine China (Eat On It)

**Song:** Fine China (Eat On It)  
**Theme:** Stop postponing joy; use the beautiful things now. Life is the occasion.  
**Vocal identity:** warm, charismatic, playful, soulful spoken-sung lead; conversational verses, stacked gang shouts in hook. Original fictional voice; no imitation of a named artist.

## Intro — spoken, intimate
There's a room in every house where nobody ever goes.  
A bottle gathering dust that nobody ever opens.  
A dress waiting for a wedding, a dream waiting for a sign.  
There's a whole lot of living waiting on the other side of someday.  
Well... someday's here.

## Verse 1
Grandma had a cabinet, glass doors and lace,  
Silverware polished, never out of place.  
Little gold plates that nobody could touch,  
Everything beautiful, but never too much.  
Had a bottle of something for a day worth remembering,  
But the years kept passing and the cork kept surrendering.  
To the dust, to the waiting, to the someday parade,  
How much of your life are you putting away?

## Pre-Chorus
Take the good stuff out, let the sunlight in.  
If it's worth keeping, it's worth using again.  
Don't wait for the guests, don't wait for the sign.  
The table is set and the moment is mine.

## Chorus — sung lead + gang responses
Don't save the fine China — **EAT ON IT!**  
Pour the good wine — **DRINK ON IT!**  
Wear your Sunday shoes on a Tuesday night.  
If you got a little love, you got a reason to celebrate life.  
Don't save it for later, don't wait in line.  
The world's too beautiful to leave it behind.  
Don't save the fine China — **EAT ON IT!**

## Verse 2
Take that old guitar down off the wall.  
Call up your people, invite them all.  
Put on that jacket that you bought last year.  
Dance in the kitchen like nobody's here.  
We spend our whole lives keeping things new,  
While time keeps moving right past me and you.  
What's the point of a treasure locked away,  
If you never get to enjoy it anyway?

## Bridge — beat strips to bass pulse; spoken
You know what?  
The good dishes are gonna get washed.  
The good clothes are gonna get worn.  
The good wine is gonna run out.  
And one day... we're all gonna leave this table.  
So pass the plates.

## Final drop — call and response
Don't save the fine China! **EAT ON IT!**  
Don't save the good wine! **DRINK ON IT!**  
Don't save your best days! **LIVE ON IT!**  
Don't wait for the right time! **THIS IS IT!**  
(repeat, stack harmonies and crowd shouts)

## Outro — spoken over fading groove
Life ain't a museum. You ain't a museum piece.  
Take the cover off the good stuff.  
And pass it down the table.
