PROJECT NOTES

LOCAL ONLY

EARTH STUDIO now uses one host-neutral musical transport for play, pause, stop, seek, tempo changes, MIDI look-ahead scheduling, and loop position. The same clock contract is designed to be reused by a future native standalone host. Audio clips remain at their original speed; time-stretching, plugin hosting, and Genesis-controlled editing are future milestones.

Projects and imported audio can now be saved locally in this browser. Browser storage can still be cleared by the operating system or user; export backups for important work. iPhone/Safari behavior and quota need real-device testing.
