# Financing Scenario Schema

A financing scenario is a **set of explicit assumptions**, not a claim that financing is available.

Required quantitative inputs:

- down payment
- closing costs
- rehab
- monthly debt service

The scenario retains a name and notes so quotes/assumptions can be traced.

Labels such as conventional, FHA-style, seller financing, or subject-to describe scenario families only. Actual loan eligibility, assumption rights, due-on-sale provisions, insurance, taxes, liens, closing requirements, and legal consequences must be verified for the specific transaction.

Base One does not silently estimate missing financing terms.
