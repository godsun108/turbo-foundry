# Stress Test v0.1

Status: preregistered

## Hypothesis

A candidate acquisition should remain inspectable when rent, vacancy, and operating-cost assumptions move against the base case.

## Grid

Default sensitivity grid:

- Rent: 90%, 100%, 110% of base estimate
- Vacancy: 5%, 10%, 15%
- Operating expenses: 100%, 115%, 130% of base estimate

## Outputs

Monthly cash flow, annual NOI, cash-on-cash return, and break-even occupancy.

The engine reports scenarios; it does not label a property a buy or sell.
